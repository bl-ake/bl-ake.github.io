<div data-lang="en" markdown="1">
This work introduces OctSurf, a novel volumetric representation based on the octree data structure to characterize 3D molecular surfaces of protein binding pockets and bound ligands. This approach significantly reduces memory usage and computational cost compared to conventional volumetric CNNs, making it tractable at higher resolutions while maintaining prediction accuracy for protein-ligand binding affinity.
</div>

本文提出 OctSurf，一种基于八叉树的层次化体素分子表面表征，用于刻画蛋白质结合口袋和配体的三维分子表面。与常规体素卷积神经网络（CNN）相比，该方法显著降低内存占用和计算成本，因此能够处理更高分辨率的输入，同时保持蛋白质-配体结合亲和力预测的准确性。
